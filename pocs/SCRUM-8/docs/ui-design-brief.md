# UI design brief

Canonical design concept for this POC. Generated by the RFP Analyst from explicit + derived UI requirements. The Developer implements this concept; they do not invent a new one while coding. Visual ideation must not add screens, roles, fields, workflows, or business rules.

- Jira key: SCRUM-8
- Source: explicit (Harvest Table tokens) + inferred composition
- Generated at: 2026-09-30

## Product identity

RecipieHub is the lasting home for recipes that today die in group chats and screenshots. Home cooks publish structured recipes; people they trust follow them and see new work in a feed. The product feels like gathering around a shared table — readable serif titles, cream paper surfaces, sage for “yes, follow” and primary actions, terracotta for the moments that matter while cooking. It is social only through follow, not comment threads or commerce.

## Explicit RFP constraints

- **Harvest Table** on sign-in, feed, recipe page, add-recipe form, cook profile: cream paper background, sage green primary actions and follow, terracotta highlights, ink-brown body text, serif titles.
- Light theme only; no dark mode; no second theme.
- Recipe fields: title, photo, servings, time, ingredients, numbered steps, optional free-text tags.
- Cook-from recipe page is in scope.
- No comments, no restaurant/grocery features.

Do not override token roles (sage = primary/follow, terracotta = highlights).

## Design opportunity

- Product is fundamentally about: durable, followable home-cook recipes and cooking from them step by step.
- Who is using it: home cooks publishing; friends/family following and cooking; occasional guests opening a shared link.
- Primary emotional goal: trust, warmth, and clarity at the stove — not performance or virality.
- Primary functional goal: find a recipe, follow a cook, cook step-by-step without hunting threads.
- Difference from a generic CRUD application: feed + follow graph, cook-from step flow, Harvest Table editorial warmth, guest-readable shared recipes.
- Real-world experience this product resembles: a handwritten recipe card on a kitchen table, Sunday supper spread, cookbook page with margin notes.
- Visual subjects that naturally belong to this domain: ingredient lists, step numbers, servings/time, cook name, typographic “dish covers,” follow ribbon—not generic dashboard widgets.
- Information that deserves visual dominance: on the recipe page, **numbered steps while cooking**; on the feed, **what’s new from people you follow**; on profile, **who this cook is and their recipes**.

## Candidate directions

### Direction A — Sunday Supper Spread

- Visual metaphor: A long table with the recipe laid open — ingredients to the side, steps down the center where your eyes travel while cooking.
- Emotional tone: Warm, communal, unhurried confidence.
- Visual personality: Cream fields with ink-brown type; sage buttons like fresh herbs; terracotta accents like clay serveware.
- Typography personality: Serif display for recipe titles; calm sans or serif body for steps and ingredients; clear step numerals.
- Color strategy: Harvest Table as specified; terracotta concentrated on cook-from highlights (step rail, active step).
- Composition / layout: Cook-from page: asymmetric two-column — narrower ingredients/metadata, wider step column; feed uses varied card heights (featured vs compact), not uniform tiles only.
- Imagery / media: TypographicCover or user-upload placeholder for recipe photo; no faux food shots.
- Interaction model: Step rail shows progress through numbered steps (visual state on active step); follow toggles with sage primary styling.
- Signature element: **Terracotta vertical step rail** beside numbered steps on the cook-from recipe layout.

### Direction B — Pantry Ledger

- Visual metaphor: A household ledger clipped to the fridge — lists, ticks, dated entries.
- Emotional tone: Practical, organized, slightly nostalgic.
- Visual personality: Ruled dividers, monospace-adjacent step numbers, tighter rows.
- Typography personality: Strong serif titles; compact list typography for ingredients.
- Color strategy: Harvest Table with terracotta used for “new in feed” markers rather than step rail.
- Composition / layout: Single-column stack; feed as dense ledger rows with left rule.
- Imagery / media: Small typographic thumbnails; initials for cooks.
- Interaction model: Expand/collapse ingredient groups on recipe page (progressive disclosure).
- Signature element: Ledger margin line with terracotta tick for “step complete” (interaction-heavy; weaker on guest cook-from clarity).

### Direction C — Market Stall Mosaic

- Visual metaphor: Stalls of dishes side by side — discovery first, cooking second.
- Emotional tone: Browsable, lively, discovery-led.
- Visual personality: Offset mosaic grid on feed; large typographic covers.
- Typography personality: Bold serif headlines; tags as handwritten-style chips (still ink/sage/terracotta).
- Color strategy: Harvest Table; sage on follow chips; terracotta on tag highlights.
- Composition / layout: Feed-forward mosaic; recipe detail opens as overlay sheet.
- Imagery / media: Large typographic covers dominant in feed.
- Interaction model: Hover/focus elevation on feed tiles; less emphasis on step-by-step rail.
- Signature element: Broken-grid feed mosaic (strong for browse, weaker for cook-from focal requirement).

## Selected direction

- Chosen: **A — Sunday Supper Spread**
- Novelty (unexpected, product-specific — not random chrome): **Pass** — terracotta step rail tied to cook-from capability is domain-native, not template chrome.
- Relevance (makes sense for this product and users): **Pass** — step rail supports cooking, the core “cook from” promise.
- Coherence (one concept, not mixed tropes): **Pass** — table/spread metaphor aligns with Harvest Table tokens and serif editorial type.
- Delight (one memorable, useful moment): **Pass** — active step on the rail reads instantly at the stove.
- Usability (readable, discoverable, accessible, responsive): **Pass** — rail degrades to inline step emphasis on narrow viewports; contrast via ink on cream.
- Feasibility (scaffold Tailwind + `components/ui`; no unapproved kits): **Pass** — CSS borders/background tokens + semantic lists; no custom animation required.
- Media availability (RFP files / approved upload / typographic or domain-native fallback): **Pass** — typographic covers per IRFP when no photos supplied.
- Scope safety (no new screens, roles, fields, or business rules): **Pass** — composition only on approved recipe/feed/profile/sign-in/form screens.

## Design metaphor

Sunday supper spread: ingredients and serving context sit to the side like bowls on the table; the recipe steps run down the center where the cook’s attention lives.

## Emotional target

Calm readiness — “I can cook this tonight” — with a sense of connection to the cook you follow.

## Visual personality

1. Cream paper surfaces with soft borders, not stark white SaaS panels.  
2. Serif recipe titles as the “dish name” anchor.  
3. Sage for affirmative actions (sign-in submit, follow, publish).  
4. Terracotta reserved for highlights and the cook-from step rail (not sprayed on every button).  
5. Ink-brown body copy for long reading (ingredients, steps).

## Composition

- **Feed:** Top app header with search entry; main column of recipe cards with rhythm (one larger “recent” card alternating with compact rows). Follow state visible on cards where applicable.
- **Cook-from recipe page:** PRIMARY = numbered steps in wide column; SECONDARY = ingredients + servings/time + tags; UTILITY = edit (author) / follow (signed-in). Terracotta **step rail** left of (or inline with) steps, active step highlighted.
- **Cook profile:** Cook name + follow control header; recipe grid/list below with typographic covers.
- **Add/edit form:** Single-column form on cream with sage submit; terracotta only for validation highlights or section labels if needed.
- **Sign-in:** Centered narrow form on cream; sage primary CTA.

## Visual hierarchy

- **PRIMARY:** Recipe title and current cooking step (cook-from); on feed, recipe title + cook on each card.
- **SECONDARY:** Ingredients block, servings/time, tags.
- **SUPPORTING:** Cook name, dates, tag chips.
- **UTILITY:** Navigation, search, edit link, unfollow.

## Imagery / media strategy

Recipe “photo” uses **TypographicCover** (title + servings/time on cream/sage/terracotta accents) unless the plan includes real upload fixtures. Cook avatars: initials tile. No stock food photography or decorative SVG meals.

## Interaction concept

On the cook-from recipe page, the **terracotta step rail** marks the active step among numbered steps (click or scroll sync if implemented; static highlight acceptable for POC if it demonstrates the rail). Follow/unfollow uses sage button styling with clear state change. Motion, if any: subtle step highlight transition only; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Signature moment

The terracotta step rail running alongside numbered steps on the cook-from layout — the user remembers **where they are in the recipe**, not “green buttons.”

## Content density

Moderate: feed shows multiple realistic recipes with varied title lengths; recipe page fully populated ingredients (8–12 items) and 5–8 steps; profile lists 4–6 recipes. Avoid empty placeholders.

## Visual rhythm

Feed alternates **large typographic cover card → compact text-forward row**; recipe page shifts from hero title/cover band → dense ingredient block → stepped narrative column; profile header band (low) → recipe grid (medium). Avoid five identical cards in a row.

## Controlled asymmetry

Cook-from page: ~35/65 split ingredients vs steps on desktop; one featured recipe card per feed “page” of fixtures offset slightly wider than neighbors.

## Responsive behavior

Below `md`, cook-from stacks: cover/title, metadata, ingredients, then full-width steps with **step rail becoming a left border or inline terracotta marker** per step (rail identity preserved). Feed becomes single column; signature rail moment still visible on recipe page. Search remains reachable from header.

## Component direction

- **Buttons:** Sage fill for primary/follow; outline ink for secondary; terracotta not used for default buttons.
- **Cards:** Cream `card` with subtle border; feed cards mix cover-led and text-led variants.
- **Badges/tags:** Optional tags as small ink-on-cream chips with terracotta dot or left border.
- **Forms:** Labeled fields on cream; serif only in titles, not in every label.
- **Empty states:** Warm copy (“Follow a cook to see their recipes here”) on cream with sage CTA to search or sign-in.
- **Navigation:** Simple top bar — RecipieHub wordmark (serif), search, sign-in/account.

## Anti-patterns (this design)

- Generic purple/blue SaaS or AI gradients.
- Dark mode or theme switcher.
- Comment threads, likes, or commerce CTAs.
- Identical three-column card grids with no cook-from focal layout.
- Faux stock food photos or random illustration blobs.
- Glassmorphism, neon, or terracotta on every interactive element.
- Dashboard sidebar unrelated to consumer recipe browsing.

## Acceptance tests

- Memorable moment: The terracotta step rail on the cook-from recipe page showing which step is active among numbered steps.
- Could this be any app if the logo and product name were removed? **No** — because Harvest Table serif+cream+sage+terracotta system, follow-based feed, and step-rail cook-from layout are specific to home recipe sharing and stovetop use, not a generic CRUD or admin UI.
